## 1. Research memo — organization facts

**The organization**
Elevate 215 is a Philadelphia education nonprofit built around Beat the Odds, which funds schools outperforming what their student population would predict. The organization also runs Apply Philly Charter, a streamlined charter application platform launched in 2018 that over 150,000 families used in the most recent fiscal year, and Philly School Compass, which helps families explore school options citywide. Funding comes through grants and donor relationships, managed day to day by the development team (Yvette).

**Scale and goals**
The organization is aiming for 50 schools, roughly 30,000 students, by 2027 — Stacy noted this number shifts depending on which internal figure you're looking at, unresolved.

**The stakeholder**
Stacy Holland, Executive Director. Owns the board relationship, the grant-making calls, and the org's scaling strategy. Not hands-on in the underlying systems herself — relies on Priya (finance), Marcus (schools index), Renée (grantee relationships), and Yvette (development) to each hold their piece.
